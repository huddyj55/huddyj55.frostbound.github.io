FROSTBOUND

A survival RPG on the arctic tundra. Every world is carved from a new seed: frozen lakes, pine forests, stone ridges and buried chests, never the same twice.

To host: start or continue a world, press Esc, then choose Invite friends.

On a phone: drag the left side to move, tap the world to use or place, hold on the world to break or attack. HIT and USE act on what you face. In the bag: tap to move items, hold to quick-move or craft all. Turn your phone sideways for more room.
